# Annual sale-price increases, 22 districts of Tehran, 1400–1405

Generated by `scripts/build_tehran_annual_price_increases.py` from the Kilid district series
(`kilid/kilid_data/…/kilid_tehran_sale_price_monthly.csv`): 22 districts × 60 consecutive months,
`1400-06` … `1405-05`, asking price per m².

```bash
python3 scripts/build_tehran_annual_price_increases.py
```

## Files

| File | Rows | What it holds |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `district_window_prices.csv` | 110 | one row per district × 12-month window: the level, quartiles, total-price median, sample sizes, and how many months were `DIRECT` vs `MODELED` |
| `district_annual_changes.csv` | 22 | one row per district: the five window levels, the four annual changes, the cumulative change, the endpoint variant, and quality columns |
| `city_summary.json` | – | method, cross-district medians/ranges, caveats, provenance, input hash |

## Method

* the series is cut into five **12-month windows**, each starting in month 06:
  `1400-06…1401-05` (W1401) → `1404-06…1405-05` (W1405). Month-06→05 keeps every window
  seasonally identical, which matters for asking prices;
* each window is one number per district: the **median of its 12 monthly district medians**;
* annual change = `W(n) / W(n-1) − 1`; cumulative = `W1405 / W1401 − 1`;
* an **endpoint** variant (`1405-05 / 1400-06 − 1`) is shipped alongside, labelled — it answers a
  different question (two specific months rather than two annual levels).

## Headline result

| change | median across 22 districts | range |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1402 vs 1401 | **+77.9 %** | +29.1 % … +141.4 % |
| 1403 vs 1402 | **+48.1 %** | +20.1 % … +87.0 % |
| 1404 vs 1403 | **+11.6 %** | +3.3 % … +22.9 % |
| 1405 vs 1404 | **+30.1 %** | +16.0 % … +59.8 % |
| cumulative W1401 → W1405 (window medians) | **+280.4 %** | +194.0 % … +348.3 % |
| cumulative `1400-06` → `1405-05` (endpoints) | **+596.6 %** | +357.8 % … +707.7 % |

The two cumulative variants differ a lot, and that difference is itself information: the
window-median version compares annual *levels*, the endpoint version compares two specific
months and is inflated by a steep run-up inside the 1405 window (and by the final month being a
provisional 3-month figure). Both are published; neither is silently preferred.

Spatially the increases are uneven, which is the analytically interesting part: districts 4, 7 and
20 roughly **+345 %, +348 %, +316 %** over the four years, while 21, 22 and 11 lag at
**+194 %, +201 %, +224 %**.

## What these numbers are — and are not

* **Asking (listing) prices**, not registered transactions and not the Statistical Center's
  transaction series. The level is an OBSERVED Kilid figure; every change here is a CALCULATED
  ratio. Nothing in this folder may be described as an official price index.
* **Nominal toman.** `data/annual.csv` (the repo's inflation series) stops at **1401**, so real
  (inflation-adjusted) changes for 1402–1405 **cannot** be computed without adding a CPI series.
  Given the rial's move over this period, a nominal "+280 %" is not a real gain.
* **Methodology risk:** Kilid's own pipeline (3-month smoothing, sample thresholds, model
  infilling) may have changed across five years; a change in coverage would masquerade as a price
  change. Five years is long enough for this to matter.
* **District 20** contains 8 `trust="MODELED"` months (`1404-08`…`1405-03`) inside its 1405
  window — its 1405 change is the least reliable of the 22. All other windows are `DIRECT`.
* Sample sizes are large (median ≈ 5,500 listings per district-month) but not uniform: the output
  carries `sample_size_median`/`_min` per window and flags any window with a month under 20.
* The final month is provisional: `windowMonths = 3` and `lastObservedPeriodCode` (`1405-06`) runs
  one month ahead of `series.lastPeriodCode` (`1405-05`).

## Independent support for the level

The Aban 1402 district table from the RTF archive (dataset 6: district average price per m²,
transcribed from an image, origin unverified) can be compared with Kilid at the same month:
**median ratio Kilid / that table = 1.06** across the 22 districts at `1402-08` (18 of 22 within
±25 %; outliers: district 18 +57 %, district 21 −27 %). That supports the *level* Kilid reports.
It says nothing about the *changes*, which is the part that depends on Kilid's method being
stable over time.

## What would make this stronger

1. a **CPI series for 1402–1405** (SCI monthly or annual) to publish real as well as nominal
   changes;
2. a **second transaction-based source** for a couple of years to validate the *trend* — the
   archive's datasets 4 and 6 are single snapshots (Tir 1403, Aban 1402) and cannot validate a
   five-year path;
3. confirmation that Kilid's methodology did not change over 1400–1405.
